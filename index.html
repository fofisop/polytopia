Aquarion

The oceanic tribe known for theri speedy attacks and their amazing life on water.

Top ten tips on how to play with this tribe:

1.Priorotize the "spearing" tech. Reaching tridentions means beingable to do chain attacks, as well as good range.

2.Get flooding! Flood waterways near enemy cities to increase your chance of stealign them over.

3.Swim out to the ocean as fast as possible. Use what Aquarions are best for, and map out the area; knowing who and where the enemies are before they know you.

4.If the enemy is building a navy, send out as many sharks as possible to swarm the enemy.

5.Place a "Jelly" down by every city which is close to danger. It's shock attack is very helpful for protecting land.

6.Explore deep sea ruins to grow quickly! This is the best thing to do if you want to grow a giant empire wit hthsi tribe.

7.Auto flood land with the crab to move faster on land while slowing down the enemy.

8.Jelly tanking. Though I already mentioned jellies in this list, I did not state yet that you can use them as a shield to protect large groups of travelers on your side.

9.If your crab attacks an enemy in a city surrounded by water, it cna push them into water. If that unit is not amphibious, it will drown instantly allowing you to claim the city.

10.Don't forget that you can build any structure directly on water tiles with aqua base. This can come in handy in the long game.

The fantasy-like tribe that views nature to be divine.

Top ten tips on how to play with this tribe:

1.Never regret not being able to chop trees. A well-placed sanctuary surrounded by forests and animals provides a permanent stream of stars and free Polytaur fodder.

2. Research forestry immediately to overwhelm enemies with polytaurs

3.Only enchant animals when you need them for a specific attack. Keeping them wild around a Sanctuary generates better long-term income.

4.Adult fire dragons deal splash damage,so rember to target the unit next to your target to hit both.

5.If you want to lead an army without spending too much, let polytaurs have the frontline with archers behind them.

6.While experiencing the "starvation" period or waiting for your dragon to grow up, prepare a shield over each city.

7.If playing for points, build sawmills and markets, as you may have an advantage here.

8.Polytaurs are build for profit! Considering that a poltaur is worth 2 stars, capturing a city with it will repay it's price in one turn. Murdering enemies with the polytaur can also give great profit.

9.Baby dragons have a dash ability, which allows them to fight while beign able to retreat.

10.Always fly over mountains or bodies of water with the dragon where regular units can't follow.

Polaris

Known for their snowy apprance and icy gliding.

Top ten tips on how to play with this tribe:

1.Rushing the ice bank is your number one priority. The faster you freeze the ocean, the faster you become the richest tribe in the game.

2. Your starting Mooni is fragile. Use its Freeze action to create a path, then move a Warrior onto that ice to protect the Mooni. Never leave a Mooni exposed on the frontline.

3. Even if you don't plan to move units toward a corner of the map, send a Mooni there just to freeze the water. Every frozen tile increases your Ice Bank income.

4. You can use ice to block enemy ships.

5. Load up your battle sled squad and send them on the ice. You won't regret it!

6. Break ice under non amphibious enemies to drown them.

7. If you have multiple Moonis, you can "chain freeze" a large area in one turn. Use this to create a sudden bridge to an enemy's "safe" island.

8. Place an ice fortress on your three most important cities, surrounded by Moonis

9. Take note that attacking a frozen unit with the ice archer will unfreeze it.

10.Combining the polarism tech with Knights leads to a speedy combo.

Cymanti

An insect themed tribe with poison effects.

Top ten tips on how to play with this tribe:

1. Open the game with a "boost"

2. Hexapods are your most important early unit. They are like glass cannons of the bug world.

3. Use Physchis for early scouting. Their wings can fly over any water or mountains!

4. When able, always attack with poison first, then attack with other forms of damage.

5. Use disposable units to go out and murder sea life to creat algae bridges.

6. Be careful with doomux explosions, as it can hurt your own troops as much as the enemy.

7. Wrap your centipide around cities!

8. If you heal the head of a Centipede, only the head recovers. To keep the whole "train" alive, you need a Shaman to boost the head, which gives the entire unit a speed/attack buff.

9. If playing against Polaris, use algae to slow them down.

10. Algae provides +1 population. This is often the only way to level up a water surrounded city enough to get a centipede.
